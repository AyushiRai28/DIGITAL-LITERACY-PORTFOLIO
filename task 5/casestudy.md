
## 🔐 Cyber Crime Case Study: The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

## 📌 Overview

The WannaCry ransomware attack was one of the most widespread cyberattacks in history. It affected hundreds of thousands of computers across the world in May 2017.

## ⚠️ What Happened?

WannaCry is a type of ransomware — malicious software that locks a user’s files and demands payment to restore access.

It spread rapidly using a vulnerability in Microsoft Windows.
The attack exploited a tool developed by the NSA (National Security Agency).
Within hours, it infected over 200,000 computers in 150+ countries.

## 🎯 Major Targets
Hospitals (UK’s NHS system was severely affected)
Banks and financial institutions
Telecom companies
Government organizations

## 💻 How the Attack Worked
The malware entered systems through a Windows vulnerability.
It encrypted files on the infected computer.
A message appeared demanding payment in Bitcoin.
Users were warned that their data would be deleted if they didn’t pay.


## 🚨 Impact
Disruption of healthcare services (cancelled surgeries, delayed treatments)
Financial losses worth billions of dollars
Loss of important data
Global panic over cybersecurity weaknesses


## 🔍 Causes of the Attack
Outdated operating systems
Lack of security updates
Weak cybersecurity practices
Unpatched vulnerabilities

##🛡️ Preventive Measures
Keep systems and software updated
Use strong antivirus and firewalls
Avoid clicking on suspicious links or emails
Regularly back up important data
Use secure networks and encryption

## 📚 Key Lessons Learned
Cybersecurity is critical for individuals and organizations
Even government-grade tools can be misused
Regular updates and awareness can prevent major attacks
Preparedness is better than recovery
